# IPR note (talk to your institute's IPR cell before filing)

Possible claims to discuss (not legal advice, and novelty must be checked in a prior-art search):
- Treating a silent or faulty sensor as a distinct warning state that can never display "all clear", combined with last-known-value risk evaluation
- Downstream risk escalation from an upstream node's rise rate, with staleness checks on the upstream data
- Tiered, multilingual advisory generation from structured reason codes, with an SMS fallback triggered only at the highest level

Steps: prior-art search (Indian Patent Office InPASS, Google Patents) -> invention disclosure form -> discuss provisional filing or a copyright registration for the software and dataset -> keep dated lab notes. Publishing before filing can destroy novelty; agree the order with your guide.
